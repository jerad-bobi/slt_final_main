Reaction quiz

Launch an endless pop-up sign quiz and stay alive by answering faster than the timer drains.

Each round shows a random SignASL video with multiple choices. Correct answers refill part of the timer, wrong answers cut it down, and the drain speeds up the longer you survive.

Rules

  • The timer bar sits above the question box and never stops draining.
  • Correct answers refill a little and move you to the next question.
  • Wrong answers reduce the timer and make recovery harder over time.

How it feels

Study mode becomes a pressure round.

The page stays calm until you start. Then the quiz appears as a centered modal, loops through unlimited questions, and ends only when the timer reaches zero.

Content source

Randomized videos from the existing SignASL integration.

The correct answer always matches the shown sign clip, while the other options act as distractors from the same curated quiz pool.
